# C-024: POST /api/v1/integrations/slack/interactions

**Status:** Frozen
**Owner:** services/gateway
**Auth:** The Slack workspace signing secret, carried by neither a bearer token nor a console session. The gateway recomputes `v0=` followed by the lowercase hex HMAC-SHA256 of `v0:<X-Slack-Request-Timestamp>:<raw body>` keyed by the secret and compares it in constant time with `X-Slack-Signature`, refusing a request whose timestamp is more than 300 seconds from its own clock. The raw received body bytes are what the signature covers. A deployment with no Slack workspace configured answers `404` on this path.
**Request:** `POST` with an `application/x-www-form-urlencoded` body whose `payload` field holds the Slack interaction JSON, and with the `X-Slack-Request-Timestamp` (epoch seconds) and `X-Slack-Signature` headers. The interaction is a `block_actions` payload carrying `user.id`, the acting Slack member, and an `actions` array holding an element whose `action_id` is `aegis_approve` or `aegis_reject` and whose `value` is an approval ticket token rendered `appr_` followed by 26 base32 characters. A member is mapped to one AegisMCP operator identity and a role set by deployment configuration; the mapped identity, never the Slack user ID, is sealed as the resolving approver. A mapped member holding `operator` or `admin` may decide; a member holding neither, or holding no mapping at all, is refused and is never trusted with an inherited role.
**Response 200:** Object. An authenticated callback resolves through the same approval path as the console (C-004, C-014). Every callback the gateway has authenticated is answered `200`, because Slack redelivers any other status, and at most one decision is applied per ticket, so a replay reports the outcome already recorded. The body carries:
- `replace_original`: boolean, `true` when the answer replaces the approval message in the channel, `false` when the message stays as posted.
- `text`: string, the outcome the channel or the acting member is shown.
- `response_type`: string, present and exactly `ephemeral` only when the answer is shown to the acting member alone; absent otherwise.

The deployment holds no interaction response URL and no bot token, so this body is the only instruction Slack receives; the endpoint answers each authenticated case as follows:
- A decision applied to a pending ticket carries `replace_original` true and `text` exactly `Approved by <operator> at <processedAt>` for `aegis_approve` or `Rejected by <operator> at <processedAt>` for `aegis_reject`, where `<operator>` is the mapped operator identity sealed as the approver and `<processedAt>` is the ISO-8601 decision time recorded on the ticket. The suspended call is released or denied before this answer, and the decision is sealed in the audit ledger.
- A ticket already approved, rejected, or cancelled carries `replace_original` true and `text` exactly `Already <outcome> by <reviewer> at <time>. No second decision was applied.`, where `<outcome>` is `approved` or `rejected`, `<reviewer>` is the identity recorded as the approver or `system` when the ticket was cancelled by the agent's stream closing, and `<time>` is the recorded decision time.
- A ticket that reached its 300-second time-to-live carries `replace_original` true and `text` exactly `Expired at <expiresAt>. The suspended call was denied. No second decision was applied.`, where `<expiresAt>` is the ISO-8601 expiry recorded on the ticket.
- A token naming no existing ticket carries `replace_original` false and `text` exactly `That approval request no longer exists.`
- An approval action whose `value` is absent or blank carries `replace_original` false and `text` exactly `This approval action carries no ticket identifier.`
- An acting member with no operator mapping carries `response_type` exactly `ephemeral` and `text` exactly `Your Slack account is not mapped to an AegisMCP operator. Ask an administrator to add it to the approval operator map.`
- A mapped member whose roles hold neither `operator` nor `admin` carries `response_type` exactly `ephemeral` and `text` exactly `Your operator role does not permit approval decisions. An operator or admin role is required.`
- A decision that could not be applied carries `response_type` exactly `ephemeral` and `text` exactly `The approval decision could not be applied.`

A refused actor leaves the ticket pending and reachable from the console queue. Expiry, cancellation, and a decision taken in the console or through a webhook never edit the posted message; they seal their outcome in the ledger and the console approval history, and the message is only changed by an answer to a click on it.
**Errors:**
- 401: The signature headers are missing; the timestamp is not an integer or is more than 300 seconds from the gateway clock; the recomputed signature does not equal `X-Slack-Signature`; the body carries no parseable interaction payload; the interaction type is not `block_actions`; the interaction carries no `user.id`; or the `actions` array carries no `aegis_approve` or `aegis_reject` action. The body is an object with `error` exactly `Unauthorized`; no ticket changes state and no ledger entry is sealed. No host, path, signature, secret, token, or exception detail appears in it.
- 404: No Slack workspace is configured for the deployment. The body is an object with `error` exactly `Not Found`.
- 405: The request method is not `POST`. The body is an object with `error` exactly `Method Not Allowed: only POST supported for Slack interactions`.
